# Training imagery and metadata

**Release status:** Not yet released.

This directory is intended to host the training imagery, metadata, and dataset documentation associated with the manuscript after publication.

Planned contents include:

- 512 × 512 image patches at 1, 2, and 4 m spatial resolutions;
- landform assignments for desert, alluvial fan, mountain, and saline land;
- training and validation splits;
- patch metadata and checksums;
- documentation of imagery sources and redistribution conditions.

Files subject to third-party licensing may be distributed through acquisition or extraction instructions rather than as raw imagery. Any released files should retain their original attribution and licensing information.
